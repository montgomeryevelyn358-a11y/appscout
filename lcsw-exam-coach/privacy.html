Privacy Policy

LCSW Exam Coach · Effective date: November 10, 2026

LCSW Exam Coach is published by Guangdong Green Shield Biotechnology Co., Ltd. The app is made by App Scout Montgomery. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

The app does not collect any data. It has no account, no server of ours, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud account. The only personal information we receive is what you send us if you choose to email us (see Reporting a problem with a question below).

What the app stores on your device

To let you study and pick up where you left off, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Your answers to practice questions, your list of missed questions, the questions you flag, and your diagnostic, practice and mock exam results.
  • A history of your answers with the time of each answer and how long it took, used to show your streak, trends and statistics on your device.
  • Your flashcard marks (which key rules you know) and when each card is due again.
  • Saved reports of your recent mock exams, which questions have appeared in your mocks, and which monthly question update you have seen.
  • Which questions Daily 5 and the free short mock have shown you, and whether the free short mock is used.
  • If you enter them: your exam date and daily goal. These are used only inside the app, for your countdown, your goal and the Next step suggestion.
  • Your reminder choice and time, if you turn on daily reminders.
  • A practice or exam you have started but not finished, so you can resume it.
  • Your text size setting and whether the small celebration buzz is on.
  • Whether the full-access purchase is unlocked on this device.
  • A few dates and counters the app uses to decide whether to show Apple's standard rating prompt (for example, the days you used the app, when it was last shown, how many questions you answered, your first diagnostic score by domain and your last mock score).

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app. We cannot see it. You can erase your answers, flags, flashcard marks and mock history at any time with Reset my progress in Settings; your purchase and settings stay, and what stays in the keychain copy is listed below. Deleting the app removes what is in its private storage. What is in your iCloud account, described below, normally stays, and so does the keychain copy that the app keeps from version 1.0.1. Version 1.0.0 keeps no keychain copy, so on a phone without iCloud or a saved file, deleting the app there deletes your progress.

A copy in your iPhone's keychain (version 1.0.1 and later)

From version 1.0.1, so that your progress can come back if you delete and reinstall the app, the app also keeps a copy in your iPhone's keychain, starting the first time you open version 1.0.1 or later: your answers, when you gave them and how long each took (the oldest may be left out if there are many), flags, flashcard marks, mock exam scores, which questions have appeared in your mocks and your Daily 5, your study plan, whether the free short mock is used, when you last reset, and the counters behind the rating prompt (the days you used the app, how many questions you answered, when and for which app version the prompt was last shown, your first diagnostic score by domain and your last mock score). Your purchase, your reports, anything you have started but not finished, your reminder, text size and buzz setting are not in it. The copy is not synced through iCloud Keychain. In a backup of this iPhone it stays encrypted and can only be restored to this iPhone. If you move straight to a new iPhone with Quick Start, it may come along with your other app data. It contains no device or user identifier, the app does not send it anywhere, and we cannot see it.

The keychain normally stays when an app is deleted, so your progress usually returns at once when you reinstall. Apple does not promise this. If you only want to reinstall, there is no need to reset first. If you want your answers out of the copy, tap Reset my progress before you delete the app, or reinstall and reset afterwards. Reset also erases your progress in iCloud and on your other devices, and it cannot be undone. It replaces the copy: your answers, flags, flashcard marks and mock history are gone from it. These stay in the copy, and in iCloud if you use it: your exam date and goal, whether the free short mock is used, when you last reset, and the rating-prompt counters listed above. If you use iCloud, the list of questions Daily 5 and the free short mock have shown stays too. Nothing in the app removes the copy completely; erasing the iPhone does.

Keeping your progress when you change phones

If you have iCloud turned on, the app syncs your progress (the same items as the keychain copy above; if you turned on daily reminders in version 1.0.0, also the reminder choice and time it saved there) through your own iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. The data goes from your device to your iCloud account and to your other devices signed in to it. It does not pass through us, and we cannot see it. If you do not use iCloud, your progress stays on the phone, and you can save it to a file and import it on another phone from Settings. That file is created by you and goes only where you choose to share it.

Reminders

Daily reminders are optional and off until you turn them on. If you do, the app asks for notification permission and schedules a local notification on your phone at the time you chose. It is created and delivered by your device. The app does not use a push notification service, and nothing about your reminder is sent to us or anyone else.

What we do not collect

The app does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access, or advertising identifier. It does not contain third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software. It does not connect to any server run by us.

Purchases

The one-time purchase and the Restore purchase function are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment and Apple ID. We do not receive your payment details or Apple ID. Apple may give us aggregated sales and download reports that do not identify you. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Reporting a problem with a question

Every question has a Report a problem link. When you use it, the app opens a draft email in your own mail app addressed to support@ludunbio.cn. The draft contains the question ID, the app version, the problem type you chose and any note you type. You decide whether to send it. If you send it, we receive the email address you sent it from, and we use the message only to review and correct the question and to reply if you ask. Please do not include personal information in your note. We keep such emails only as long as needed to handle the report.

Rating prompt and links

The app may ask for an App Store rating using Apple's built-in prompt, which Apple controls. Links in the app (for example to this policy, the Terms of Use or the App Store review page) open outside the app in your browser or the App Store, where those services' own policies apply.

This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics or set cookies on it. GitHub, as the host, may log visits under its own privacy statement.

Third-party software

The app is built with open-source libraries (React Native and Expo) and uses Apple's StoreKit for purchases. These run on your device. The app does not use them to send information to us or to any third party.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for a professional licensing exam. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. If a future version of the app collects any new information, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
