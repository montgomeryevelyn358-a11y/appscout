Privacy Policy

LCSW Exam Coach · Effective date: October 28, 2026

LCSW Exam Coach is published by Guangdong Green Shield Biotechnology Co., Ltd. The app is made by App Scout Montgomery, a small independent studio. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

We do not collect your personal information. The app has no account, no server of ours, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

To let you study and pick up where you left off, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device only:

  • Your answers to practice questions, your list of missed questions, the questions you flag, and your diagnostic, practice and mock exam results.
  • A history of your answers with the time of each answer and how long it took, used to show your streak, trends and statistics on your device.
  • Your flashcard marks (which key rules you know) and when each card is due again.
  • Saved reports of your recent mock exams, which questions have appeared in your mocks, and which monthly question update you have seen.
  • If you enter them: your exam date and daily goal. These are used only for the countdown and goal on your home screen.
  • Your reminder choice and time, if you turn on daily reminders.
  • A practice or exam you have started but not finished, so you can resume it.
  • Your text size setting and whether the small celebration buzz is on.
  • Whether the full-access purchase is unlocked on this device.
  • A few dates and counters the app uses to decide whether to show Apple's standard rating prompt (for example, the days you used the app and when it was last shown).

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app. We cannot see it. You can erase your study data at any time with Reset my progress in Settings, and deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Keeping your progress when you change phones

If you have iCloud turned on, the app syncs your progress (answers, flags, flashcard marks, mock exam scores, your study plan and the counters behind the rating prompt) through your own iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. The data goes from your device to your iCloud account and to your other devices signed in to it. It does not pass through us, and we cannot see it. If you do not use iCloud, your progress stays on the phone, and you can save it to a file and import it on another phone from Settings. That file is created by you and goes only where you choose to share it.

Reminders

Daily reminders are optional and off until you turn them on. If you do, the app asks for notification permission and schedules a local notification on your phone at the time you chose. It is created and delivered by your device. The app does not use a push notification service, and nothing about your reminder is sent to us or anyone else.

What we do not collect

The app does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access, or advertising identifier. It does not contain third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software. It does not connect to any server run by us.

Purchases

The one-time purchase and the Restore purchase function are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment and Apple ID. We do not receive your payment details or Apple ID. Apple may give us aggregated sales and download reports that do not identify you. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Reporting a problem with a question

Every question has a Report a problem link. When you use it, the app opens a draft email in your own mail app addressed to support@ludunbio.cn. The draft contains the question ID, the app version, the problem type you chose and any note you type. You decide whether to send it. If you send it, we receive the email address you sent it from, and we use the message only to review and correct the question and to reply if you ask. Please do not include personal information in your note. We keep such emails only as long as needed to handle the report.

Rating prompt and links

The app may ask for an App Store rating using Apple's built-in prompt, which Apple controls. Links in the app (for example to this policy, the Terms of Use or the App Store review page) open outside the app in your browser or the App Store, where those services' own policies apply.

This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics or set cookies on it. GitHub, as the host, may log visits under its own privacy statement.

Third-party software

The app is built with open-source libraries (React Native and Expo) and uses Apple's StoreKit for purchases. These run on your device. The app does not use them to send information to us or to any third party.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for a professional licensing exam. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. If a future version of the app collects any new information, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
