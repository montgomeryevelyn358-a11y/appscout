LCSW Exam Coach: Support

LCSW Exam Coach is a clinical reasoning coach for people preparing for the ASWB Clinical (LCSW) exam. It gives you case-vignette practice questions in the style of the exam format that starts in August 2026, explains the principle behind every answer, and shows why each wrong option is wrong. You can take a 20-question diagnostic, practice by domain and topic, flag questions, study key-rule flashcards, review your mistakes, track your progress, and sit a timed full-length mock exam.

It is a one-time purchase with no subscription, and it works offline.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a subscription? Will I be charged again?

No. Full access is a single one-time purchase made through the App Store. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore purchase. Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. Restoring does not charge you again.

Where is my progress stored? Do I need an account?

There is no account. Your answers, mistakes, flags, results, exam date, goals and settings are stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud account. We have no server and cannot see them. With iCloud turned on, a new phone signed in to the same Apple ID picks up your progress. Without iCloud you can save your progress to a file in Settings and import it on the new phone. Deleting the app on a phone without iCloud or a saved file deletes its progress.

I think a question or an explanation is wrong. How do I report it?

On any question, tap Report a problem with this question. Choose what is wrong, add a note if you like, and send. It opens an email to us that you review and send yourself. You can also use Report a problem in Settings.

How fast do you fix mistakes?

We review every report within 48 hours. Fixes reach you in the next app update.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase. We cannot issue refunds ourselves.

Is this the official ASWB app? Will it predict my score?

No. LCSW Exam Coach is an independent study aid, not affiliated with or endorsed by ASWB. The practice questions are original, and practice results do not predict your result on the real exam.
