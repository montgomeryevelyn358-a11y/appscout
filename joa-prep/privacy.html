Privacy Policy

JOA Prep · Effective date: 30 October 2026

JOA Prep is an unofficial practice app for the Job Opportunities Assessment, made by App Scout Montgomery, a small independent developer, and published on the App Store by Guangdong Green Shield Biotechnology Co., Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

We do not collect any data. The app has no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your progress is stored only on your device.

What the app stores on your device

So you can practise and pick up where you left off, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device only:

  • Your answers in practice, mocks and sprints, whether each was right, and how long each question took. These drive your results, the mistakes list, your daily session and your progress charts.
  • Your mock results, a mock or practice session you have started but not finished, and your best sprint score.
  • If you enter them: your test date and how many minutes a day you want to practise. They are used only for the countdown and the size of each day's session.
  • Your settings: whether the mock clock shows minutes left, the appearance (system, light or dark), and whether you have seen the welcome pages and the tour.
  • Whether Full Access is unlocked on this device.
  • A few dates and counters the app uses to decide when to ask for Apple's standard rating prompt (for example, the days you opened the app and when the prompt was last requested).

The app never sends this data to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

What we do not collect

The app does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or advertising identifier. It does not ask for any device permissions. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not connect to any server run by us.

Purchases

The one-time Full Access purchase and Restore purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment and your Apple ID. We do not receive your payment details or your Apple ID. Apple may give us sales reports that do not identify you. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Email support, only if you write to us

If you use Report a problem, the app opens a draft email in your own mail app addressed to support@ludunbio.cn. The draft contains the question ID, the question bank version, the reason you chose and any note you type. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

If you email us, for a report or anything else, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you and to fix the question, keep them only as long as needed for that, and never share or sell them. Please do not include personal details you do not want us to have.

Rating prompt and links

The app may ask iOS to show Apple's built-in rating prompt, which Apple controls and may choose not to show. Links in the app (this policy, the Terms of Use, the App Store review page and the official guide on the ADF Careers website) open outside the app, where those services' own policies apply.

This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics, no cookies and no third-party fonts or scripts. GitHub, as the host, may log visits under its own privacy statement.

Children

The app is meant for people preparing to apply to the Australian Defence Force. It is not directed to children. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. If a future version of the app ever collects any information, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
